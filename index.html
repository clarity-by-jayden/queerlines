Oye

by Melissa Mogollon

A humorous, heartfelt debut told through a series of one-sided phone calls from Luciana, an 18-year-old Colombian American, to her older sister away at college. When a hurricane hits Miami and their eccentric grandmother receives a shocking medical diagnosis, Luciana must step up as caretaker.
