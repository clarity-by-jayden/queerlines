Author Spotlight: Josh Silver (Fruit Fly)

If you like your fiction sharp, funny, and quietly devastating, this month’s author spotlight is for Josh Silver — the writer behind Fruit Fly, our Queer Between the Lines Book #007.

Josh’s work has a knack for taking big, thorny themes — identity, authorship, addiction, power, belonging — and turning them into stories that are readable, tense, and darkly entertaining, without losing the emotional truth underneath.

Why we’re spotlighting Josh for Book #007

Fruit Fly is the kind of novel that makes you pause mid-chapter just to think, “Hang on… what is this really saying?” It plays with perception and storytelling in a way that feels perfect for a QBTL discussion — messy, smart, and very much not here to give you neat answers.

A few places to find Josh

  • Penguin Random House author page: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/2297601/josh-silver/
  • Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/joshsilverauthor/

If you vibe with…

  • stories about reinvention and self-mythology
  • books that question who gets to tell the story
  • dark humour + psychological tension
  • characters who are complicated (and sometimes infuriating)

…Josh’s writing will likely give you plenty to chew on.

Question to bring into the discussion

Who gets believed — and what does it cost to keep being “the version of yourself” that other people want?
